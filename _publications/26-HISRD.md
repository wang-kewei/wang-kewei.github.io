This preprint extends spherical-radial decomposition to infinite-dimensional stochastic settings by combining a subspace method with Monte Carlo sampling. The resulting approach provides unbiased, low-variance estimates and derivatives of probabilistic functions, with applications to chance-constrained stochastic PDE optimization and constrained Gaussian process hyperparameter learning.
